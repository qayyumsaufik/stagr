About

{{ w }}

The name

Stagr takes its name from the stag. An antler is a record of the years behind it, thickened and scarred and branched, and none of it is covered up. We ask the same of leather. It should show its age rather than hide it.

We cut in Pakistan, from hides we choose ourselves. One workshop, a small bench, no subcontracting. A belt leaves us when it is right, not when the week ends.

Prices run from Rs 1,740 to Rs 3,500. That is the whole range, and it is the same for a gift of one or an order of a hundred.
