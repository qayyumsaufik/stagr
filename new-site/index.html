How it is made

One hide, four hands, no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    The material

    We buy whole hides and reject about a third. What is left is graded by hand for grain, thickness and stretch.

  2. 02

    The cutting

    Straps are cut along the spine where the hide is tightest, so a belt holds its shape instead of curling.

  3. 03

    The stitching

    One row of saddle stitch, two needles, waxed linen thread. It cannot unravel the way a machine seam can.

  4. 04

    The finish

    Edges bevelled, sanded, burnished four times, sealed with beeswax. Then the buckle goes on and it is checked.
