06/Story

We cut leather, not corners.

We cut in Pakistan, from hides we choose ourselves. One workshop, a small bench, no subcontracting. Prices run from Rs 1,740 to Rs 3,500.

Chapter 01·The name

The name.

Stagr takes its name from the stag. An antler is a record of the years behind it, thickened and scarred and branched, and none of it is covered up. We ask the same of leather. It should show its age rather than hide it.

Chapter 02·The material

The material.

We buy whole hides and reject about a third. What is left is graded by hand for grain, thickness and stretch. We cut in Pakistan, from hides we choose ourselves. One workshop, a small bench, no subcontracting. A belt leaves us when it is right, not when the week ends.

Chapter 03·The cutting

The cutting.

Straps are cut along the spine where the hide is tightest, so a belt holds its shape instead of curling.

Chapter 04·The stitching

The stitching.

One row of saddle stitch, two needles, waxed linen thread. It cannot unravel the way a machine seam can.

Chapter 05·The finish

The finish.

Edges bevelled, sanded, burnished four times, sealed with beeswax. Then the buckle goes on and it is checked. Prices run from Rs 1,740 to Rs 3,500. That is the whole range, and it is the same for a gift of one or an order of a hundred.
